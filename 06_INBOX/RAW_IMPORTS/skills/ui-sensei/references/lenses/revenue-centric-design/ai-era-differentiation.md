## Principle: "Same competes on price, different on category" (applied to AI-native products specifically)

- **Apply when:** positioning a product in a category where AI-generated capability is quickly becoming table stakes (most competitors can now claim "AI-powered").
- **The move:** in a market where the underlying capability commoditizes fast, differentiation has to shift toward mechanism, workflow integration, or trust/data ownership — not the fact of having AI features at all, since that stops being a meaningful claim once most competitors have it too.
- **Evidence:** this is the general positioning principle from `positioning-icp-and-gtm.md`, applied specifically to the AI-tooling landscape rather than SaaS positioning broadly — same underlying logic, narrower application.
- **Visual:** messaging built around a specific workflow outcome ("turns your meeting notes into filed tickets automatically") rather than a generic AI-capability claim ("powered by advanced AI").
