# Style: Industrial Brutalist (adapted from leonxlnx/taste-skill — brutalist-skill)

A raw, mechanical interface direction mixing Swiss print-grid structure with terminal/military-inspired aesthetics. Where `minimalist.md` is quiet and editorial, this source is quiet in a *different* way — stripped of ornament, but dense and utilitarian rather than airy.

## Core visual rules

- **Visible grid structure.** Swiss-grid layout logic made explicit rather than hidden — columns, rules, and alignment should be legible, not softened away.
- **Monochrome or near-monochrome, data-dense surfaces.** Reads closer to a terminal, cockpit display, or CRT readout than a consumer app — high information density is the point, not a compromise.
- **Typography does the heavy lifting**, bold and structural, often mono or condensed grotesque faces — no decorative type.
- **No stock-photo imagery.** If images appear, they should feel documentary/technical, not lifestyle marketing.
- **Minimal, functional motion only** — this isn't a kinetic style; movement should feel mechanical/utilitarian (a hard cut, a snap-into-place), not fluid or organic.

## When this style works best

Data-dense interfaces, developer tools, technical/industrial products, editorial content with a serious or austere tone. **Explicitly not suited** to consumer apps or anything wanting to feel warm/friendly — the source itself flags this as a hard mismatch, not just a stylistic preference. If the request signals warmth, approachability, or a consumer-friendly tone, don't offer this as a candidate at all in Step 3's preview — prefer `minimalist.md` (if the tone should still be quiet/restrained) or `frontend-design.md` (if it should feel warmer and more conventional) instead.

## Note on combining with other sources

The source repo warns that its own family of aesthetic skills (this one, `minimalist.md`, and others) weren't designed to be stacked together — loading more than one at once produces undefined blending behavior. That warning was specifically about combining skills *within this family*; treat it as suggestive, not proof, when applied more broadly to `SKILL.md`'s Step 5 precedence rule for combining any two sources in this curation — the two claims are related but not identical in scope.

**Unverified:** unlike the flagship skill (which had a documented v1→v2 rewrite), it's not confirmed whether `brutalist-skill` itself went through similar revisions. Re-check at population time rather than assuming stability.
