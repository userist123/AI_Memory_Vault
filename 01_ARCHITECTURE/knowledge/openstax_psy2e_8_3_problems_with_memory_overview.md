## 2. Concepte Extrase și Validate Verbatim

### 1. Amnesia
Amnesia refers to a loss of long-term memory that occurs as the result of disease, physical trauma, or psychological trauma. The text identifies two distinct types of amnesia that are subject to comparison.

> "Compare and contrast the two types of amnesia"

### 2. Eyewitness Testimony
Eyewitness testimony is a legal and psychological concept regarding the accuracy of memory recall for events witnessed by individuals. The text highlights that such testimony is often considered unreliable.

> "Discuss the unreliability of eyewitness testimony"

### 3. Encoding Failure
Encoding failure occurs when information is not successfully processed into memory in the first place. This represents one of the fundamental reasons why memory retrieval may fail.

> "Discuss encoding failure"

### 4. Interference
Interference is a phenomenon where the retrieval of memory is hindered by other information. The text notes that there are two distinct types of interference to be compared and contrasted.

> "Compare and contrast the two types of interference"
