## 2. Concepte Extrase și Validate Verbatim

### 1. Rehearsal
Rehearsal is a memory-enhancing strategy involving the conscious repetition of information. This process helps facilitate the transfer of information from short-term memory to long-term memory.

> "One strategy is rehearsal , or the conscious repetition of information to be remembered (Craik & Watkins, 1973)."

### 2. Chunking
Chunking is a strategy used to improve memory by organizing information into smaller, manageable bits or units. It is particularly effective for remembering sequences like phone numbers or dates.

> "Another strategy is chunking : you organize information into manageable bits or chunks (Bodie, Powers, & Fitch-Hauser, 2006)."

### 3. Mnemonic Devices
Mnemonic devices are memory aids designed to help organize information for encoding. They are especially useful for recalling complex sequences such as steps, stages, or lists.

> "Mnemonic devices are memory aids that help us organize information for encoding ( Figure 8.18 )."

### 4. Expressive Writing
Expressive writing is a technique that can boost short-term memory capacity. Research indicates that writing about traumatic experiences for specific intervals can lead to measurable improvements in memory capacity.

> "The researchers found that this simple writing task increased short-term memory capacity after five weeks, but only for the participants who wrote about traumatic experiences."

### 5. Verbalization Effect
Saying words aloud or mouthing them increases the distinctiveness of the information, which improves memory recall. Studies have shown that this technique can increase memory for specific items by more than 10%.

> "A series of studies (MacLeod, Gopie, Hourihan, Neary, & Ozubko, 2010) found that saying a word out loud improves your memory for the word because it increases the word’s distinctiveness."
