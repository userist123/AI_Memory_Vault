## 2. Concepte Extrase și Validate Verbatim

### 1. Hippocampus
The hippocampus is a brain structure critical for memory processing, including recognition and spatial memory. It functions by projecting information to cortical regions to provide meaning and facilitates the consolidation of new learning into long-term memory.

> "They concluded that the hippocampus is involved in memory, specifically normal recognition memory as well as spatial memory (when the memory tasks are like recall tests)"

### 2. Memory Consolidation
Memory consolidation is the biological process of transferring new learning into long-term memory. Damage to the hippocampus disrupts this process, resulting in an inability to form new declarative memories.

> "It also plays a part in memory consolidation: the process of transferring new learning into long-term memory."

### 3. Patient H.M.
Patient H.M. underwent a bilateral removal of his hippocampi to treat seizures, which resulted in severe anterograde amnesia. While he retained memories from before the surgery, he lost the ability to form new declarative and semantic memories.

> "He lost the ability to form new memories, yet he could still remember information and events that had occurred prior to the surgery."
