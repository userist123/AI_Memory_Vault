## 2. Concepte Extrase și Validate Verbatim

### 1. Retrieval
Retrieval is the cognitive process of accessing information from memory storage and bringing it into conscious awareness. It is essential for daily functioning, enabling tasks ranging from basic self-care to complex professional activities.

> "The act of getting information out of memory storage and back into conscious awareness is known as retrieval ."

### 2. Recall
Recall is a method of memory retrieval that involves accessing information without the assistance of external cues. It is commonly required in academic settings, such as when answering questions on an essay test.

> "Recall is what we most often think about when we talk about memory retrieval: it means you can access information without cues."

### 3. Recognition
Recognition is the process of identifying previously learned information upon encountering it again. This method relies on a comparison process and is typically utilized during multiple-choice examinations.

> "Recognition happens when you identify information that you have previously learned after encountering it again."

### 4. Relearning
Relearning involves the process of acquiring information that was previously learned at an earlier time. This method often results in faster acquisition of the material compared to initial learning.

> "The third form of retrieval is relearning , and it’s just what it sounds like. It involves learning information that you previously learned."
