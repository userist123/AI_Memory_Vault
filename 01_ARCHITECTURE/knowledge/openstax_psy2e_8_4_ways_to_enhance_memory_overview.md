## 2. Concepte Extrase și Validate Verbatim

### 1. Memory-enhancing strategies
These are techniques designed to improve the ability to retain and recall information. They are intended to mitigate common memory failures, such as forgetting the location of personal items or academic material.

> "Recognize and apply memory-enhancing strategies"

### 2. Effective study techniques
These are structured methods used to optimize the learning process. They are specifically aimed at helping individuals better retain information required for academic assessments.

> "Recognize and apply effective study techniques"
