## 2. Concepte Extrase și Validate Verbatim

### 1. Amygdala
The amygdala is a brain structure primarily responsible for the regulation of emotions, including fear and aggression. It plays a critical role in memory formation by influencing how memories are stored, particularly when events are emotionally arousing.

> "The main job of the amygdala is to regulate emotions, such as fear and aggression"

### 2. Memory Consolidation
Memory consolidation is the biological process by which new information is transferred into long-term memory. The amygdala facilitates this process by helping to encode memories more deeply when an experience is emotionally significant.

> "the process of transferring new learning into long-term memory."

### 3. Lateral Amygdala
The lateral amygdala is a specific region within the amygdala identified as the site responsible for the storage of fear memories. Research indicates that inducing cell death in this area can cause established fear memories to fade or become extinct.

> "the lateral amygdala, which is the specific area of the brain responsible for fear memories."
