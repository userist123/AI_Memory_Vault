## 2. Concepte Extrase și Dovezi Textuale Verificate

### 1. Amnesia
Amnesia refers to the loss of long-term memory caused by disease, physical trauma, or psychological trauma.

> **Citat Verificat Sursă**:
> "Amnesia is the loss of long-term memory that occurs as the result of disease, physical trauma, or psychological trauma."

- **Termeni cheie**: amnesia, long-term memory, trauma

### 2. Anterograde Amnesia
Anterograde amnesia is a condition where an individual cannot remember new information following trauma, although memories formed prior to the injury remain accessible.

> **Citat Verificat Sursă**:
> "With anterograde amnesia , you cannot remember new information, although you can remember information and events that happened prior to your injury."

- **Termeni cheie**: anterograde amnesia, consolidation, short-term memory

### 3. Retrograde Amnesia
Retrograde amnesia is characterized by the loss of memory for events and personal history that occurred prior to experiencing trauma.

> **Citat Verificat Sursă**:
> "Retrograde amnesia is loss of memory for events that occurred prior to the trauma. People with retrograde amnesia cannot remember some or even all of their past."

- **Termeni cheie**: retrograde amnesia, episodic memories

### 4. Memory Construction and Reconstruction
Forming new memories involves construction, while retrieving old memories involves reconstruction, a process during which memories can be altered, modified, and distorted.

> **Citat Verificat Sursă**:
> "The formulation of new memories is sometimes called construction , and the process of bringing up old memories is called reconstruction . Yet as we retrieve our memories, we also tend to alter and modify them."

- **Termeni cheie**: construction, reconstruction, retrieval

### 5. Suggestibility
Suggestibility refers to memory distortion occurring when misinformation from external sources leads to the formation of false memories.

> **Citat Verificat Sursă**:
> "Suggestibility describes the effects of misinformation from external sources that leads to the creation of false memories."

- **Termeni cheie**: suggestibility, misinformation, false memories

### 6. Misinformation Effect Paradigm
Developed by Elizabeth Loftus, the misinformation effect paradigm demonstrates that exposure to post-event, inaccurate information causes individuals to misremember the original event.

> **Citat Verificat Sursă**:
> "Loftus also developed the misinformation effect paradigm , which holds that after exposure to additional and possibly inaccurate information, a person may misremember the original event."

- **Termeni cheie**: misinformation effect paradigm, Elizabeth Loftus, eyewitness testimony
