Corp

10–12 September 2027Timișoara, Romania · Friday to Sunday

Corp is a contemporary dance festival where the body is the whole subject. Three days, four venues and eighteen performances, from the Opera stage to a courtyard in the Fabric district.

Get tickets

Programme

Eighteen shows over three days. Pick a day, or see everything at once. Most evening shows run about 60 minutes without an interval.

Venues

Four places within a twenty-minute walk of Piața Victoriei. The festival pass covers all of them.

Teatrul Național

Piața Victoriei 2

The main stage. A proscenium hall for the large ensemble works and the opening and closing nights.

Main hall · 640 seats · step-free entrance

Opera Română

Strada Mihai Viteazul 2

The Opera's studio floor hosts the late programme, close to the audience and sprung for floorwork.

Studio · 180 seats · stairs, lift available

Fabrica courtyard

Fabric district

A former factory yard, used for open-air shows and the Sunday afternoon workshops. Bring a layer for the evenings.

Outdoor · 300 standing or on benches · free access

Piața Victoriei

City centre

The opening procession and one free public performance take place on the square itself, under the arcades.

Outdoor · open to everyone · no ticket needed

Tickets

Choose a pass or single shows. Children under 12 enter free to Sunday's family show with an adult.
