Programme

Three days, one body.

Each evening moves from an intimate studio piece to a large-scale premiere, with late sets and open-air work in between. Times are local (EEST).

Good to know

Before you come.

How do I get to Timișoara?

Timișoara Traian Vuia Airport has direct flights from major European cities. The Gara de Nord station connects to Bucharest, Budapest and Vienna. Trams 1 and 9 reach the centre.

Is the festival suitable for children?

The Sunday family performance is made for ages 5 and up. Evening works are recommended for 14+; look for the age note in the programme.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are refundable up to 7 days before the festival. After that they can be transferred to another person by email.

Are there English surtitles?

Most works are non‑verbal. Spoken pieces carry English and Romanian surtitles, and talks are bilingual.
