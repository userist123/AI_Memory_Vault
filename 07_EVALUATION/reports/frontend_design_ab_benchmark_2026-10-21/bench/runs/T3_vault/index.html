    Getting here

    Timișoara Traian Vuia airport is 12 km from the centre (bus line 6 and taxis). Timișoara Nord station is a 10-minute tram ride from Piața Victoriei.

    Language

    Most works are non-verbal. Spoken pieces and talks are in English or Romanian, as marked in the programme.

    Late arrivals

    Doors close when the performance starts. Latecomers are admitted at the stage manager's discretion.
