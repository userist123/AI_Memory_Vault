Programme

Twelve works across three days. Pick a day; each venue is listed beside the piece. Outdoor events are free.

Venues

All four venues sit within a twenty-minute walk of Piața Victoriei. Programme is subject to change.

A

Main Stage Hall

The festival's principal theatre: proscenium stage, raked seating, full lighting rig. Home of the evening premieres.

Near Piața Victoriei · 480 seats · step-free access
B

Studio Thalia

A black-box studio for solos, duets and work in progress. Close quarters, no bad seats.

Old Town · 120 seats · step-free access
C

The Brewery Floor

A converted brewery hall with concrete floor and tall windows. Late sets, standing audience, bar open.

Fabric district · 300 standing · ramp entrance
D

Bastion & Square

Open-air stages by the old fortress walls and on the square. Free, with no seating and no tickets.

Citadel quarter · free entry · weather permitting

Tickets

Prices in RON, VAT included. Students, pensioners and under‑18s get the reduced rate with ID at the door.
