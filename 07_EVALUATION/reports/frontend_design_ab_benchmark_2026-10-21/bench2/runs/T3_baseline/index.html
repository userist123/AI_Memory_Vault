Good to know

Getting around

All venues are within a 20-minute walk of Piața Victoriei. Tram lines 1, 2 and 4 serve the centre.

Access

Every venue has step-free access. Relaxed performance on Sunday at 14:00; contact us for assisted seating.

Language

Most works are non-verbal. Spoken pieces are surtitled in Romanian and English.
