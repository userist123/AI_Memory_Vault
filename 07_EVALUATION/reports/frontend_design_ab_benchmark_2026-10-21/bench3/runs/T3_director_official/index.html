Fourteen works, one weekend

Evening shows at 19:00 and 21:30, daytime studies and talks in between. Filter by day or venue.

02 — Venues

Four rooms, ten minutes apart

Everything sits inside the historic centre and can be walked between shows. Doors open 30 minutes before each performance.
