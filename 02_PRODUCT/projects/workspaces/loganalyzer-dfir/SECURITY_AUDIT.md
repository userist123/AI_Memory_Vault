## Verdict

**Production ready: NO, pending two owner decisions.** Acceptable for the current **local-testing stage** on controlled stations. Every code finding of this audit is fixed with tests (#1–#9, #11–#20; research backlog #3 and #4), and so are the six findings of the independent review of those fixes (V1–V6) and the five of the review of the remediations (W1–W5), see "Findings fixed". The remaining blockers for a production / accreditation release are owner decisions, not code:

1. **#10 Authenticode:** an OV/EV code-signing certificate, signing in CI and a signed `SHA256SUMS`; the startup check of native libraries (#5, manifest embedded in the executable since V3) does not replace a signature on the executable itself.
2. **#21 Legacy source trees** (`_recovered/`, root `Services/`, `ViewModels/`, `LogAnalyzer.UI/AirGapped/Network.csproj`): archive branch or deletion.

Owner action already recorded under #3: generate the offline licence key (`license-keygen`) and compile its public key in, otherwise every licence is refused (fail closed).
