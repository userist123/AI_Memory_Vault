Config panel

Each editable variable needs validation and a human explanation. The UI must prevent dangerous settings, not just accept them.

RISK_PCT
float · allowed 0.001–0.02
Validated
Junior meaning

This is the fraction of account equity risked if the stop loss is hit.

Senior meaning

Used inside lot sizing: lot = (equity × risk_pct) / (sl_ticks × tick_value), then rounded to broker step.

XAU_SPREAD_MAX_RATIO
float · guard rail
Context aware
Junior meaning

Stops the bot from entering when spread is too wide versus normal market conditions.

Senior meaning

Entry blocked when live spread exceeds rolling mean spread × ratio, preventing low-quality fills.
