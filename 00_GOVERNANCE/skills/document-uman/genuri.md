# Genre templates (Romanian professional documents)

Research 2026-11-22 (two web agents, 26 YouTube transcripts, two blind reviews; sources in
found source. Laws: always re-check the current text with skill `analist-juridic` / `surse-web` before
citing an article in a real document.

## Universal craft rules
- Main message / request first (BLUF, Minto). Reader must know in the first 3 lines what is asked.
- Pyramid test: read ONLY the first paragraph + headings; the message must survive.
- One subject per paragraph, paragraphs under ~10 lines; sentences avg 15-20 words, none over 40.
- Active voice, doer named. Imperative for procedures. Present tense for rules.
- Nominalisations back to verbs; cut "în vederea", "în scopul de a", "la nivel de", "cu privire la"
  (-> "despre/privind" once), "procedura de efectuare a".
- Numbers: sums in figures and words in contracts/notices: "5.000 lei (cinci mii de lei)"; "o mie",
  not "una mie". Dates 22.11.2026 or 22 noiembrie 2026.
- Close with one concrete next step + deadline + contact.
- Edit in separate passes: structure -> sentences -> proofread; one issue per pass; read aloud
  (render and reread the PNG as the cooled-off reader).
- Pleonasms to kill: perioadă de timp, a reveni înapoi, a anticipa dinainte, perspectivă de viitor,
  procent la sută, deplină unanimitate, a urca în sus, notorietate publică, panaceu universal.
- "de asemenea" (never "deasemenea"). Diacritics always. "dumneavoastră" lowercase is correct;
  capitalised is courtesy in letters - pick one, stay consistent.
- Calques to avoid: "a adresa o problemă" (-> a rezolva/a trata), "a face sens" (-> are sens),
  "a aplica pentru" (-> a candida/a depune cerere), "locație" for place (-> loc/sediu), "oportunitate"
  for chance, "reminder" (-> memento / v-am reamintit).
- Not too ceremonial: never stack "prin prezenta ... vă rog respectuos să binevoiți". One formula.
- Clear text reads as MORE credible to experts. Write contracts and notices for the
  parties who must perform them, not for an imaginary judge; drop archaic filler with no legal effect
  - but keep Romanian operative formulas (pact comisoriu wording, "în temeiul art. ...").

## Lessons from blind review, round 1
- If the authority has a standard form, use it or reproduce every field: certificat de urbanism =
  formular F.1 (Ord. 839/2009, anexa 1 - verify current version); ANAF, ONRC, procurement forms likewise.
  Search the institution's website for "formular" before drafting free text.
- Never sample identifiers: IBAN bank code must match the bank (BTRL = Banca Transilvania, BRDE = BRD,
  RNCB = BCR, INGB = ING, RZBR = Raiffeisen); VAT code "RO12345678" no space; real data or a placeholder.
  Fictional test data must still pass checksums (CNP, CUI, IBAN mod 97).
- Dates on working days for outgoing mail, invoices, deadlines; due date on weekend moves to next
  working day (art. 2554 C. civ.). Romanian holidays count.
- Sums in words: from 20 up take "de": "patruzeci de lei", "o sută de lei"; 1-19: "cincisprezece lei".
- No colon after "În atenția", "Referitor la" (they govern the next word): "În atenția doamnei Elena Pop".
- "Nr. 418/22.11.2026" - no spaces around the slash, same form everywhere in the document.
- Debt recovery between professionals: money debt is in default of right at due date (art. 1523
  alin. (2) lit. d) C. civ.); if an ordonanță de plată is planned, send a SOMAȚIE (art. 1014 CPC;
  art. 1015 = the court application): term 15 days, via executor or recomandată cu conținut declarat
  și confirmare de primire. State the communication method in the document. Verify before use.
- Consumer debtor (private person): art. 1523 alin. (2) lit. d) does NOT apply (professionals only);
  put an "întârziere de drept" clause in the contract (art. 1523 alin. (1)) or send a notice first.
  Consumer contracts: competent court is the consumer's domicile court, mention ANPC, pact comisoriu
  operates only after notice; avoid unfair terms (Legea 193/2000). Verify before use.
- Signer must have power to represent (administrator / legal representative) or a mandate ("în baza
  împuternicirii nr. ..."); always leave signature space and "L.S." for stamp where customary.
- One grammatical person per letter (company letter = "noi" throughout). One word per thing
  (do not alternate "materiale"/"mărfuri").
- Offers to public authorities: price without VAT, VAT (21% standard rate since 01.08.2025 - verify),
  total with VAT, payment term (Legea 72/2013), transport/unloading included or not, CPV code,
  validity, title "OFERTĂ", annex fișa tehnică.
- One letterhead per company, identical in all documents, placed in the Word header (`docx_ro.antet`).
- Salutation "Stimată doamnă," / "Stimate domnule director," is more natural than "Stimată doamnă Pop,".
- Do not add sentences the genre does not need (justifications nobody asked for).

## Lessons from blind review, round 2
- Name the contract by its legal nature: installation with own materials = contract de ANTREPRIZĂ
  (art. 1851 urm. C. civ.), not "prestări servicii". Sale = vânzare, etc.
- Consumer (B2C) contracts: state that the commercial warranty does not affect the legal conformity
  guarantee (OUG 140/2021) and give the pre-contract information (OUG 34/2014); say where it is signed
  (off-premises contracts trigger 14-day withdrawal). Penalty clauses must be proportionate - the
  judge checks unfair terms ex officio even in ordonanța de plată (Legea 193/2000).
- Cash: never offer cash payment above the legal cap (Legea 70/2015, individuals -> companies;
  verify current cap) and never suggest splitting a payment to avoid it.
- Proces-verbal de RECEPȚIE: signatures by role ("Executant", "Beneficiar"), not AM PREDAT/AM PRIMIT
  (that is for predare-primire). Name the authorised person (ANRE installer + authorisation no.) when
  the work requires one. No academic caption numbering for a single table.
- Every referenced document has a number and date ("procesul-verbal de remediere nr. 115/19.10.2026").
- Letters end with place and date ("Brașov, 24.11.2026") and a signature line.
- Trade-register number: check the current ONRC format (Legea 265/2022 introduced a new format) -
  copy it exactly from the company's certificate, do not invent the old J00/000/0000 pattern.
- Numbering plausible for the firm's size (invoice no. vs contract no. vs PV no.).
- Wording: "în următoarele condiții" (not "cu respectarea clauzelor de mai jos").

## Lessons from YouTube round 2 (13 videos)
Official RO
- Salutation names the office, not the person: "Domnule Primar,", "Doamnă Director,", "Domnule
  Președinte," (capitalised office; all caps "DOMNULE PRIMAR," is also accepted). Never "Domnule
  Popescu," in a cerere.
- Leave the top of the page free for registration and rezoluție (school norm ~10 cm; in practice at
  least a quarter of the page).
- Closing: date on the left and signature on the right, on the SAME line, after the text; bottom
  line = recipient's function and institution.
Letters
- Three-paragraph core: why I write / the details / exactly what the reader must do now ("vă rog să
  ... până la ..."), never "vă rog să rezolvați situația". Max one page.
- Neutral tone in notices: no "inacceptabil", "pretindem", "vina dumneavoastră"; state the legal
  consequence plainly.
- Signature gap 3-4 lines above the typed name, room for a handwritten signature. A short letter
  starts lower on the page instead of leaving 3/4 blank; no bigger font to fill. Count enclosures
  ("Anexe: 3"); "cc:" only when copies are really sent.
- Do NOT import US/UK letter format: no colon after the salutation, no "Sincerely" equivalents, no
  month-first dates.
Reports
- Readers jump to recommendations: recommendations and executive summary must stand alone; the
  summary is a separate section before the body. Findings are interpreted ("ce înseamnă"), not raw
  data. If the purpose is to recommend, recommendations come before the conclusion.
E-mail
- Subject = action + object + time: "Aprobare deviz încălzire - 5 minute, până joi" [1XctnF7C74s,
  Hkh_surdw74]; 2-6 words, never "Important". One topic per e-mail/thread. Request first, context
  after, even to seniors. Due date in the first sentence. Say at the top when you add/remove
  recipients. Summarise a messy incoming e-mail before answering it. Check the To: field.
Contracts
- Allocate every foreseeable risk explicitly: "if X happens, who bears it and who pays" - unclear
  clauses are where disputes start; tailor to the actual job, never paste a stock contract
  unchanged; technical facts written so a non-technical judge understands.

## Cerere / petiție to a public institution (OG 27/2002)
Reply term 30 days from registration (+ max 15 extension). Anonymous / no identification = filed away.
```
[free space at the top for registration number and rezoluție - about a quarter of the page]
                         Domnule Primar,            (or "DOMNULE PRIMAR," / "Doamnă Director,"; centered)

Subsemnatul [Nume Prenume], domiciliat în [localitate], str. [..] nr. [..], [bl., sc., ap.],
județul [..], CNP [..], legitimat cu CI seria [..] nr. [..], telefon [..], e-mail [..],
vă rog să [one clear request, verb: aprobați / eliberați / dispuneți].

[Motivation: facts in chronological order, short; legal basis with exact article if relevant.]

Anexez:
1. [document]
2. [document]

Data: 24.11.2026                                              Semnătura,

DOMNULUI PRIMAR AL MUNICIPIULUI [..]   (bottom line, dative, function + institution)
```
- One request per petition.

## Notificare / punere în întârziere (art. 1522 Cod civil) / Somație (art. 1014 CPC)
Must give a term for performance; communicated by executor or any means proving delivery
(scrisoare recomandată cu confirmare de primire). For ordonanța de plată: somație, 15 days,
executor or recomandată cu conținut declarat și confirmare de primire.
```
NOTIFICARE (punere în întârziere)  /  SOMAȚIE

Către: [debitor, identification, address]
De la: [creditor, identification]

Vă notificăm să achitați suma de [x] lei ([în litere]), reprezentând [factura nr. ../data,
contractul nr. ../data], scadentă la [data], în termen de [n] zile de la primirea prezentei.
[Facts: contract, delivery, invoice, partial payments, previous reminders - dates.]
Temei: art. [..] [+ contract clause].
Dacă suma nu este achitată în termen, vom introduce acțiune în instanță pentru recuperarea
debitului, a penalităților de [..] și a cheltuielilor de judecată.
Plata se poate face în contul [IBAN], deschis la [bancă].
Comunicare: [scrisoare recomandată cu conținut declarat și confirmare de primire / executor].
Anexe: 1. ... 2. ...
[Localitate], [data]          [Firma], prin [administrator], semnătura, L.S.
```
Note: if suing without prior notice and debtor pays within the term, creditor bears court costs
(art. 1522 alin. (5)).

## Plângere prealabilă (Legea 554/2004 art. 7)
To the issuing authority or its superior; within 30 days from communication of an individual act;
asks "revocarea, în tot sau în parte" of [act nr./data]. Normative act: anytime. Missing it ->
court action rejected as premature. Structure: petent identification, actul atacat (nr., data, emitent,
data comunicării), motive de nelegalitate (fapt, drept), solicitare (revocare), anexe, data, semnătura.

## Contract
Mandatory blocks: title by legal nature + nr./data; PĂRȚILE (company: denumire, sediu, CUI, nr. ONRC,
IBAN, reprezentant + funcție; person: nume, CNP, CI serie/nr., domiciliu); OBIECTUL; PREȚUL ȘI
MODALITATEA DE PLATĂ (figures + words); DURATA; OBLIGAȚIILE PĂRȚILOR (each duty on a named party,
active voice, "are obligația să" / "va"; consistent defined terms); RĂSPUNDEREA (clauză penală
calibrated); FORȚA MAJORĂ (art. 1351); ÎNCETAREA (incl. pact comisoriu "de plin drept, fără
intervenția instanței și fără punere în întârziere" - B2B only); NOTIFICĂRI (addresses/channels);
CONFIDENȚIALITATE; LITIGII (amiabil, apoi instanța competentă); CLAUZE FINALE (modificare doar prin
act adițional; "Prezentul contract s-a încheiat astăzi, [data], în 2 (două) exemplare originale,
câte unul pentru fiecare parte."); signatures (`docx_ro.semnaturi`).
Articles numbered "Art. 1", sub-points "(1)", "a)". Headings bold caps or "CAPITOLUL I".
Risk allocation explicit for every foreseeable event.

## Proces-verbal de predare-primire [U for closing]
"Încheiat astăzi, [data], ora [..], la [adresa], între: [predător] și [primitor]."
Table: nr. crt. | denumire | cantitate | stare / observații. Defects noted explicitly.
"Drept pentru care s-a încheiat prezentul proces-verbal în 2 (două) exemplare."
Two-column signatures: AM PREDAT, / AM PRIMIT, with names.
(Proces-verbal de RECEPȚIE: signatures by role Executant / Beneficiar - see round 2 lessons.)

## Proces-verbal de ședință / minută
Date, place, attendees + absentees, quorum, ordinea de zi; per item: discussion (short, past tense,
3rd person, neutral), decision stated precisely with vote count; action items = verb + task + owner +
deadline; date of next meeting; signatures (președinte de ședință, secretar).

## Decizie internă [U]
"DECIZIA nr. [..] din [data]" | issuer + representative | "Având în vedere: ..." (facts/documents) |
"În temeiul art. [..] din [lege/act constitutiv]," | "DECIDE:" | "Art. 1. ..." numbered |
last article: who carries it out + communication | signature, ștampilă.

## Referat / notă internă
Header Către / De la / Data / Subiect; first paragraph = what you propose; then situation, options,
costs; "Propun:" ; space for "APROB," (top right, head of unit) and "Avizat".

## Raport (business)
Title page only if > 5 pages. Rezumat (≈10%, answer first, standalone) -> Context -> Constatări
(headings as statements: "Costurile cu energia au crescut cu 18%", each interpreted) -> Recomandări
(ordered by importance, each with owner/term; standalone) -> Concluzii -> Anexe. Intro does not state
findings. Tables/figures numbered only when there are several.

## E-mail profesional
Subject = what the reader must do ("Solicitare: confirmarea comenzii 4512 până vineri").
Salutation "Bună ziua, doamna Ionescu," is accepted in neutral business email; formal: "Stimată
doamnă,". Request in the first paragraph. Short paragraphs. Close: "Cu stimă," + name, function,
company, phone. Never "tu" with an official. No social-media links, no "cu cele mai calde salutări".

## Demisie (Codul muncii) [pRHkpm4tSyU; verify art. 79-81 C. muncii before use]
Unilateral act: NEVER "vă rog să aprobați demisia" - the employer cannot refuse and no approval is
needed. Content: employee identification, employer, function, "îmi prezint demisia din funcția de ...
ocupată în baza contractului individual de muncă nr. .../...", preaviz of N zile lucrătoare (legal
maximum 20 for execution, 45 for management positions - verify; or the shorter term in the CIM), last
working day, date, signature. Delivery: registered at the employer with number on the copy, or post
with confirmare de primire, or e-mail only if agreed; keep proof of delivery. Resignation without
notice only if the employer breached its obligations - then state the reasons in writing.

## Refusals / bad news
Neutral buffer -> reasons -> clear refusal -> alternative -> polite close without over-apologising.

## Dosar AGA asociație de proprietari (Legea nr. 196/2018) [blind review 2026-11-22, round 3]
- Contents, in this order, listed in an OPIS with page ranges computed from the parts: opis,
  convocator, raport de activitate și situația financiară, raportul cenzorului (short, first person,
  signed and dated by the cenzor, ends with the proposal on approval and descărcare de gestiune),
  extras din registrul de calcul (Excel), centralizatorul ofertelor, formular de împuternicire.
  Everything the convocator says is available must be in the dossier.
- Agenda = one list in the data model, used by the convocator, the "propuneri supuse votului" in the
  report and the vote table of the proxy form. It includes approval of the report, the cenzor's
  report, descărcarea de gestiune, the budget for next year (with the repairs-fund contribution),
  every decision the report proposes, elections when mandates expire; "Diverse" without vote.
- Genre title: "Raport de activitate și situația financiară pe anul X" + "Situație la <data>"; ONE
  cut-off date for expenses, lists, payments, balances, penalties and cash. Later events are not
  mixed in; say what the annual closing will add.
- Contributions to the repairs fund are not expenses ("cheltuit"); show them as a fund movement.
  Water is split by meter consumption when apartments have meters (repartitoare are for heating;
  for water the words are contoare / apometre, contoare de control pe coloane).
- Arrears = balance minus lists not yet due; age from the due date of the oldest unpaid list.
  Penalties per unpaid list from its due date, 0,2 %/day after 30 days past due, capped at the list
  (art. 77 L. 196/2018); payment imputation per art. 1509 C. civ. (medium confidence - say which
  rule was applied). Court mandate threshold: 90 days past due (art. 77).
- Cash reconciliation table: fond de rulment + facturi încă neplătite - sume de încasat (fără fondul
  de reparații) +/- diferențe din rotunjire = disponibil = extras + registru de casă. Never write
  "nu avem datorii" without this table; write "nu avem facturi restante" and show what is unpaid.
- Notice: no "afișat cu N zile înainte" sentence on the convocator itself; the number and date at
  the top are enough (check >= 10 days in code). Same font family in every part of the dossier.
