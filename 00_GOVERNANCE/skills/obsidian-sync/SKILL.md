# Obsidian Synchronization

Obsidian is the visualization, navigation, and human-audit layer over the Vault.

## Rules

- Canonical content remains in the Vault's designated knowledge/project/procedure/system areas.
- Update relevant MOCs and links when canonical records are created or changed.
- Avoid duplicate copies of the same durable knowledge solely for Obsidian navigation.
- Preserve existing frontmatter and Vault conventions when editing notes.
- Do not modify `.obsidian` configuration unless the task explicitly requires configuration changes.

## Linking

Use stable relative links and existing MOCs where possible. Prefer linking to canonical records rather than raw external imports.
