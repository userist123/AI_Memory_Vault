
#### Detailed Findings by Modality:
1. **1-Hop Wikilinks & Relations**:
   - Direct lexical recall was only **19.67%** because notes linking to one another often use complementary or non-overlapping vocabulary (e.g. `Import Pipeline` $\to$ `Promotion and Human Review`).
   - 1-hop expansion achieved **68.85% recall**, rescuing 37 notes (**60.66% rescue rate**).
2. **2-Hop Neighborhoods**:
   - Lexical recall dropped sharply to **8.62%** over 2-hop distances.
   - 2-hop expansion jumped to **67.24% recall**, rescuing 35 notes (**60.34% rescue rate**).
3. **Entity-Mediated Projections**:
   - Shared technical identifiers (e.g. `P0-015`, `SHA-256`, `BM25`, `SQLite WAL`) connected 456 pairs.
   - Multi-hop traversal rescued **111 notes** that lexical search alone missed.
